# RunCat animal runners

Artwork by **Takuto Nakamura (Kyome22)**.

Source: https://github.com/runcat-dev/RunnerGallery

License: Apache License 2.0 (full text in `LICENSE`).

Retrieved on 2026-11-14 from `runners/<id>/preview.png` and `metadata.json`:
`classic-cat`, `rabbit`, `welsh-corgi`, `shiba-inu`, `fox`, `chicken`.

For Qingdao, the original transparent APNG frames were arranged horizontally into
static PNG sprite sheets without changing their silhouettes. The renderer applies
a jade color mask and scales the frames to a 32 × 24 px icon slot. Original frame
timing is 100 ms. No external image service is required at runtime.
